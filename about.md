<p>I’m Kenneth Ho, an applied ML practitioner and data scientist in Singapore with ~9 years of experience building and auditing machine learning systems under adversarial and high-consequence conditions.</p>

<p>Most recently, I’ve served as Lead Data Scientist at GovTech Singapore (public-sector applied AI), leading a team of 5–6 data scientists on geospatial computer vision, air-gapped MLOps, and production-readiness evaluation audits. Previously, I led Web3 risk analytics and strategy at Binance, built anti-money laundering and refund-fraud detection systems at ByteDance (TikTok Live), and developed computer vision and forecasting systems for smallholder agriculture at Yara Digital Farming. I hold a BSc (Hons) in Physics from the National University of Singapore (Special Programme in Science) and co-authored research in <a href="https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.nanolett.6b02111">ACS Nano Letters</a>.</p>
